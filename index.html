Getting started

  • Move: up, down, left, right; aim with the mouse.
  • Lock on: toggles targeting the nearest enemy.
  • Fight: to attack (hold for a heavy strike); tap to deflect, or hold to block. A precise deflect within 50 ms is a perfect parry that staggers foes. Use for your combat art.
  • Evade: Tap to dodge; evade an attack in the first 60 ms to trigger a perfect dodge counter and leave an afterimage. Hold while moving to sprint.
  • Ascend: Death preserves your EXP. Defeat the Ashen Daimyo and John Java descends from the heavens to grant a glowing Buddha form, with 140 ms perfect parries and 100 ms perfect dodges. The blessing persists through saves and New Game +, but does not affect duels.
  • Explore: at safe shrines to open the shrine menu, upgrade skills, and fast travel; to rest without opening the menu. Use to heal and for equipment.
  • Learn: for pause options. Customize all controls in Settings.
