Getting started

  • Move: WASD or arrow keys; aim with the mouse.
  • Lock on: C or middle mouse toggles targeting the nearest enemy.
  • Fight: Left-click to attack; time right-click or K to deflect. A precise deflect within 30 ms is a perfect parry that staggers foes.
  • Evade: Tap Space or L to dodge; evade an attack in the first 50 ms of your dodge to trigger a perfect dodge counter and leave an afterimage that distracts the attacker. Hold while moving to sprint.
  • Explore: Press E at safe shrines to rest, upgrade skills, and fast travel to discovered shrines. Use Q to heal and Tab for equipment.
  • Learn: Press Esc for the full controls and pause options.
