# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Extended enemy stun after a parry to 1.2 seconds, doubled to 2.4 seconds for perfect parries.
- Difficulty now changes enemy tactics: higher tiers react faster, read attacks more effectively, punish openings, flank, and coordinate group pressure.
- Death no longer removes EXP.
- After the Ashen Daimyo falls, John Java descends from the heavens and grants a glowing Buddha form with 140 ms perfect parries and 100 ms perfect dodges. The blessing persists through saves and New Game + and is shared in co-op, but never affects duels.
- Added a customizable quick-rest action (B by default) at safe shrines, restoring supplies without opening the shrine menu.
- Breaking the player's guard no longer deals health damage; the stagger opening and vulnerability to follow-up attacks remain.
- Extended the perfect parry window from 30 ms to 80 ms.
- Combat arts now use only their dedicated button; attacking while holding block performs a normal attack.
- Reduced the reach and arc of broad perilous enemy sweeps and shortened their active hit windows and trails to 60-70 ms.
- Living camp defenders return to their original camp positions on resurrection; defeated enemies remain dead.
- Players can now time a parry during a dodge without interrupting the roll.
- Added a persistent Settings menu with master volume and mute, graphics options, and fully customizable keyboard and mouse bindings for combat, movement, menus, and multiplayer shortcuts.

- The main-menu Recent updates panel now reads this changelog directly instead of displaying a separate, outdated list.
- Renamed the easiest journey difficulty to Colton, preserving older saves and preferences.
- Reorganized the shrine menu into Sanctuary and Fast Travel categories with destination cards, pagination, and responsive hover and press feedback.
- Removed sword clashing, including the elite blade-lock QTE and its related combat states, UI, and tutorial text.
- Aligned Oni heavy attack effects with their hitbox reach and arc throughout lunges, and corrected sweep-edge collision checks.
